Privacy Policy

1. Local-First Execution: Atelier Prompt Engine processes all images, prompts, and profiles locally inside your browser. Reference files are compressed on-device via HTML5 Canvas and are never transmitted to external servers.

2. Zero Tracking: This application contains no advertising pixels, third-party analytics trackers, or behavioral profiling cookies.

3. User Data Sovereignty: You can export all workspace records as a portable JSON file or erase browser storage at any time from Settings.

Terms & Conditions

1. Permitted Use: Atelier Prompt Engine is provided as a prompt authoring and creative direction workspace for personal and commercial visual production workflows.

2. Intellectual Property: You retain full ownership of every prompt, brief, and reference image created or imported into your workspace.

3. Third-Party Generators: Prompts formatted for Midjourney, Flux.1, or Nano Banana pipelines are subject to the terms of those respective image generation platforms.

Release Notes — v1.0 Public Release

• Vision Studio: Dual Look + Outfit reference slots with client-side image compression and persistent Physique, Hairstyle, and Pose directive profiles.

• Raw Prompt Architecture: Nano Banana 2.1 Raw Synthesizer Mode, reference-led outfit transfer, natural fabric physics simulation, and anti-shrinkwrap negative additions.

• Visual Lab: Eight modular prompt sections with granular selection dropdowns, custom text overrides, and automated wardrobe conflict detection.

• Multi-Model Compiler: Instant export formatting for Nano Banana 2.1 (RAW & Master Labeled), Natural Prose, Midjourney v6, Flux.1, and Structured JSON.
